------------START------------
1. Pick starting blessing and leave the encounter (is leaving necessary? check doc)

------------MAP INTERACTION------------
2. View map (can I?)
3. Pick encounter

------------VIEW STUFF------------
4. View deck
5. View enemy state (hp, statuses, intention)
6. View player state (HP, energy)
7. View Draw pile and Discard pile and Exhausted pile
8. View gold amount
9. View Potion slots
10. View relics
11. View card 

------------FIGHT------------
12. Pick action/card
13 -> 12. Choose what enemy to attack
14. Perform action/play card
15. Use potion
16. End turn

------------END OF FIGHT------------
17. Pick rewards

------------UNKNOWN------------
18. View choices
19. Pick a choice

------------MERCHANT------------
20. Enter Shop
21. View wares
22. Buy something
23. Card Removal service
    - Check Cards 
    - Pick card to remove
24. Exit Shop

------------REST------------
25. Check available options
26. Pick either option
27. Card Upgrade
    - Check cards available
    - Upgrade card
